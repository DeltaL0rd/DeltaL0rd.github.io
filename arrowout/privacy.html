Arrow Out – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Arrow Out (“the game”, “we”, “us”) is a casual puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. We keep it as small as we can: there are no accounts, no chat and no social features.

1. Who we are

The game is published by Studio 010 (the “developer”). Contact: raovikas603@gmail.com.

2. Information that stays on your device

The game saves your progress (current level, solved levels, coins, hints, daily-reward streak) and your settings (sound, music, vibration, notifications, ad choice) in a small file on your device. We do not receive this file. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

3. Ads and the information they use

Arrow Out is free and is supported by advertising (banner, interstitial and optional rewarded video ads). To show ads we use Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) as an ad-mediation service together with advertising networks such as Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, these providers may collect and use:

  • your device’s advertising ID (Google Advertising ID) and device identifiers;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version, network type);
  • ad interaction information (ads shown, clicks, rewards, ad performance and revenue).

They use this to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see them, prevent fraud and, if you agree, personalize ads. These providers act under their own privacy policies:

  • Google (AdMob, User Messaging Platform): policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity LevelPlay / ironSource: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

We do not sell your personal information in exchange for money and we do not receive your name, email address or any other direct identifier from these providers.

4. Your ad choices

  • Personalized or non-personalized ads. On first launch we (or, in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent form) ask whether you agree to personalized ads. If you do not agree you still see ads, but they are not based on your advertising ID profile.
  • Change your mind any time. Open Settings in the game: use the “Personalized ads” switch, or “Privacy choices” where Google’s consent form applies.
  • Device settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules a small number of local reminders (for example twice a day and when a daily reward is available). They are created on your device; no personal information is sent to a server for this. You can turn them off in the game’s Settings or in your Android system settings.

6. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where data protection law such as the GDPR applies, we and our ad partners rely on your consent for personalized advertising and for storing or accessing information on your device, which you can withdraw at any time as described above. Showing non-personalized ads, measuring them and preventing fraud rests on the legitimate interest of keeping the game free and secure.

7. Retention and transfers

We do not keep personal information about you ourselves. Data processed by ad providers is kept according to their policies. Providers may process data in countries other than your own, including outside the EEA and UK, using safeguards such as standard contractual clauses where required.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and similar laws) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent and to lodge a complaint with your data-protection authority. Because we hold no personal data about you, most requests have to be directed to the ad providers listed above (for example through Google’s My Ad Center). You can also email us at raovikas603@gmail.com and we will help as far as we can. California residents: you may opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information by choosing non-personalized ads in Settings.

9. Children

Arrow Out is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at raovikas603@gmail.com.

10. Security

Ad requests are sent by the ad providers’ SDKs, which use industry-standard protections. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we only use reputable providers and collect as little as possible.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a meaningful way we will update the date above and, where required, ask for your choice again inside the game. Please review it from time to time.

12. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: raovikas603@gmail.com
