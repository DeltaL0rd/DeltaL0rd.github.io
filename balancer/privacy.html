Privacy Policy for Balancer

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Developer: Delta Game Studio ("we", "us", "our")

App: Balancer (Android package: com.DeltaGameStudio)

Contact: raovikas603@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Balancer game ("the App") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

  • Balancer does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal details.
  • Your game progress (best score, coins, unlocked skins, sound, music and vibration settings) is stored only on your device.
  • The App is free and shows advertisements. Ads are delivered through the Unity LevelPlay mediation platform, which connects the App to ad networks such as Unity Ads, ironSource Ads and Google AdMob. We may add other ad networks, such as AppLovin and Meta Audience Network, in the future (see section 3).
  • These ad services may collect certain device information, such as your advertising ID and IP address, to show and measure ads.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves the following data locally on your device so the game works between sessions:

  • Best score and coins
  • Unlocked and selected player skins
  • Sound, music and vibration preferences
  • Counters used to decide when to show an ad or a rating prompt

This data stays on your device. We do not receive it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its storage.

3. Advertising and ad mediation

The App displays banner, interstitial and rewarded video ads. To do this it uses Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service operated by Unity (and ironSource, which is part of Unity). LevelPlay runs an auction between several ad networks and shows the ad from the network that wins. The ad networks currently used or that may be used are listed below.

3.1 Information collected by ad services

To serve, measure and improve ads, and to prevent fraud, LevelPlay and the ad networks may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / Google Play services ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it (such as country or city)
  • Device and operating system details (model, manufacturer, OS version, language, screen size, battery and network type)
  • App information (version, package name) and how you interact with ads (impressions, clicks, ad completion, rewards)
  • Diagnostic, crash and performance information
  • Identifiers and interaction data linked to your device, used to show ads that are more relevant to you (personalised ads), or less relevant ads (non-personalised ads)

We do not receive your personal information from these services. We only receive aggregated reports about ad performance and earnings.

3.3 Your choices for ads

  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation on your Android device: Settings > Privacy > Ads (the exact path varies by device).
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), ad services may limit the ads shown or show only non-personalised ads unless you have given consent.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional: you choose whether to watch one to revive or to earn free coins.

4. Permissions

The App may request the following Android permissions:

  • Internet / network state: required to load and display ads.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): used by the ad services described above.
  • Vibrate: used for haptic feedback if you have vibration turned on.

The App does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or precise location.

5. Third-party links

The App contains a "Contact Us" button that opens our Discord community in your browser, and a rating button that opens the Google Play Store. These services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

6. Children's privacy

Balancer is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we do not knowingly show personalised ads to children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help resolve the issue.

7. Data retention and deletion

We do not store your personal data on our servers. Data stored in the App can be deleted at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it. Data processed by Unity, ironSource, Google and any other ad network is retained according to their own policies. You can manage Google's data through your Google account's ad settings: https://adssettings.google.com

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the App and rely on the security of the Android platform and the ad services we use. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International users

Unity, Google and other ad networks may process information in countries other than your own. By using the App you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in those countries, which may have different data protection rules than your country.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to its processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not collect personal information ourselves, most of these requests are best handled through your device settings and the privacy tools of the ad services listed in section 3.2. You can also contact us at the address above and we will do our best to help.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add or remove an ad network. The latest version will always be available at the privacy policy link on the App's Google Play Store page, and the effective date at the top will be updated. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
