This Privacy Policy explains what information the Balancer game ("the App") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

  • Balancer does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal details.
  • Your game progress (best score, coins, unlocked skins, sound and vibration settings) is stored only on your device.
  • The App shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain device information to show and measure ads (see section 3).
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves the following data locally on your device so the game works between sessions:

  • Best score and coins
  • Unlocked and selected player skins
  • Sound and vibration preferences
  • Counters used to decide when to show an ad or a rating prompt

This data stays on your device. We do not receive it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its storage.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial and rewarded video ads. AdMob is operated by Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / Google Play services ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and operating system details (model, OS version, language, screen size)
  • App information (version, package name) and how you interact with ads (impressions, clicks, ad completion)
  • Diagnostic and performance information

Google may use this information to show you ads that are relevant to you (personalised ads) or less relevant ads (non-personalised ads), to prevent fraud, and to measure ad performance. We do not receive your personal information from Google; we only receive aggregated reports about ad performance and earnings.

For more information on how Google uses data, please read:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • AdMob and Google Ads data practices: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

Your choices for ads

  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation on your Android device: Settings > Privacy > Ads (the exact path varies by device).
  • Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), you will be asked for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional: you choose whether to watch one to revive or to earn free coins.

4. Permissions

The App may request the following Android permissions:

  • Internet / network state: required to load and display ads.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): used by AdMob as described above.

The App does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or precise location.

5. Third-party links

The App contains a "Contact Us" button that opens our Discord community in your browser, and a rating button that opens the Google Play Store. These services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

6. Children's privacy

Balancer is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help resolve the issue.

We do not store your personal data on our servers. Data stored in the App can be deleted at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it. Data processed by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's own policies; you can manage it through your Google account's ad settings: https://adssettings.google.com
