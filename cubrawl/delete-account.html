Cubrawl: delete your account and data

Developer: Studio010 · Contact: raovikas603@gmail.com

Cubrawl does not have its own sign-up or password. To play online you sign in with Google Play Games, and the game creates an online player ID with Unity Gaming Services so you can join rooms. You can remove your data in either of these ways.

1. Delete data on your phone (instant)

  • Open Android Settings > Apps > Cubrawl > Storage > Clear data, or uninstall the game.
  • This removes your player name, settings, owned items shown on the device, Room Cards and free-card counters.
  • One-time purchases (for example No Ads and skins) can be restored from Google Play later. Room Cards are consumable and cannot be restored.

2. Ask us to delete your online player data

  1. Email raovikas603@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Cubrawl data".
  2. Write the player name you used in the game and the email of the Google account you used to sign in.
  3. We will confirm and process your request within 30 days. We will ask the service providers that run online play to remove the player ID linked to you.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: your online player ID and the player data linked to it, and anything you sent us by email once your request is done.
  • Kept by others: purchase records are kept by Google Play under Google's own rules, and may be kept for legal and tax reasons. We never receive your card details.

Google account and Play Games data

Your Google Play Games profile is managed by Google. You can delete Play Games data at Google Play Games or in your Google Account settings.

See also our Privacy Policy for the full details.
