Privacy Policy for Cubrawl

Effective date: 13 November 2026
Developer: Studio010 ("we", "us", "our")
Contact: raovikas603@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game Cubrawl (the "Game") collects, how we and our partners use it, and what choices you have. By playing the Game you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Game.

1. Who the Game is for

Cubrawl is a general-audience game. It is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us at raovikas603@gmail.com and we will help remove it.

2. Information we and our partners collect

We keep data collection to what the Game needs to work. We do not ask you to create an account with us, and we do not ask for your real name, address or phone number.

2.1 Information you provide

  • Player name. The name you type in Settings. It is shown to other players in matches, on leaderboards and in rooms. Please do not use your real name or any personal information.
  • Room name. If you give a private room a name, other players in that room can see it.
  • Emotes. You can send short preset reactions. There is no free-text chat in the Game.
  • Messages to us. If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write.

2.2 Sign-in (Google Play Games)

If you sign in with Google Play Games to play with real players online, Google Play Games Services gives the Game a player identifier and your Play Games display name. We use this only to sign you in and to run online play. We do not receive your Google password.

2.3 Online play (Unity Gaming Services)

Online rooms (Quick Play and private rooms) are run with Unity Gaming Services (authentication, multiplayer sessions and relay servers). To make this work, the following is processed:

  • a player identifier created by the service;
  • your player name, the room's mode, the game version and the room code;
  • your IP address and connection data, which are needed to connect you to other players through relay servers;
  • in-match cosmetic choices (your skin, face, trail, effect, name colour, badge) and emotes, which are sent so other players see them.

Matches are played in real time. We do not keep a long-term record of your matches on our own servers.

2.4 Purchases (Google Play)

You can buy items in the Game (for example Room Cards, No Ads and skins). Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment card or bank details. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made (product and transaction identifiers) so that we can give you the item. What you own is saved on your device (see 2.7). Free Room Cards are earned by watching a rewarded video.

2.5 Advertising

The Game shows ads (a banner, and a full-screen ad between some matches) and optional rewarded videos. Ads are provided through Unity LevelPlay, which may show ads from partners such as Google AdMob and Unity Ads. These services may collect and use:

  • your advertising ID (Google Advertising ID on Android);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and software information (device model, operating system, language, screen size, network type);
  • information about how ads were shown and whether you interacted with them.

They use this to show and measure ads, to prevent fraud, and, where you have agreed, to personalise ads. You can remove ads in the store by buying No Ads (rewarded videos you choose to watch remain optional).

2.6 Consent and ad choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region where the law requires it, the Game asks for your consent to ads through Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time with the "Privacy & ad choices" button in the Game's Settings (shown where consent applies). You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

2.7 Information stored on your device

The Game saves information on your device, for example: your player name, settings (music, sound, difficulty), your chosen game mode, the items you own and which ones you wear, your Room Card balance and your free-card counters. This stays on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers. If you uninstall the Game or clear its data, it is removed (purchases of one-time items can be restored from Google Play; Room Cards bought as consumable items cannot be restored).

2.8 Technical and diagnostic information

Our service providers may automatically collect basic technical information such as crash reports, device model, operating system version and the game version, to keep the Game working and to fix problems.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • let you play, sign in, join rooms and see other players' names and cosmetics;
  • give you the items you buy or earn and restore purchases;
  • show ads and measure their performance, and respect your consent choices;
  • keep the Game secure, prevent cheating and abuse, and fix bugs;
  • answer your questions and comply with the law.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Advertising partners may use advertising data as described in section 2.5.

4. Who we share information with

  • Other players in your room see your player name, room name, cosmetics and emotes.
  • Service providers that run the Game for us: Google (Google Play Games Services, Google Play Billing, Google AdMob, Google User Messaging Platform) and Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services, Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads, and their ad network partners). They process data under their own terms and privacy policies:

- Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Unity: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
- Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

  • Authorities or other parties if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our players and the Game.
  • A buyer or successor if the Game is sold or transferred. We will tell you if your information would be handled under a different policy.

5. International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own, including countries whose data-protection rules may differ. Where required, they use safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

6. How long we keep information

We do not run our own player database, so we do not keep your data on our servers. Information held by our service providers is kept as long as their own policies allow. Information on your device stays until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Change your name in Settings at any time.
  • Ads: use "Privacy & ad choices" in Settings (where shown), reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, or buy No Ads.
  • Sign out of online play in the Online menu. You can manage Google Play Games data in your Google account settings.
  • Delete your data: uninstall the Game or clear its storage to remove everything on your device. To ask about data held by us or our providers, email raovikas603@gmail.com.

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the United Kingdom or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data-protection authority. Email us to use these rights. We will reply within the time the law requires. We do not discriminate against anyone for using their privacy rights.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, and online play uses the secure connections provided by our service providers. No method of storing or sending data is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Third-party links

The Game may contain links, for example to Google Play. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other websites or services.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Effective date" at the top shows when it last changed. If we make important changes we will tell you in the Game or on the store page. Continuing to play after a change means you accept the updated policy.
