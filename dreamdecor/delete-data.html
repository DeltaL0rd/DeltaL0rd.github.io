Dream Decor has no accounts, so we do not hold a profile with your name or email. This page explains how to delete the data connected to your use of the Game.

How to request deletion

  1. Delete data stored on your device: uninstall Dream Decor, or open your phone's Settings → Apps → Dream Decor → Storage → Clear data. This removes your game progress, outfit, settings and purchase records from the device.
  2. Reset your advertising ID: on Android, open Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID. This separates earlier advertising data from your device.
  3. Ask us to delete any data we or our service providers hold: email raovikas603@gmail.com with the subject Dream Decor data deletion request. Please include your device model and, if you have it, your Google Play order number, so we can find any records. We reply within 30 days.

What is deleted and what is kept

Game progress, outfit, settings (stored on your device)Deleted when you uninstall or clear app data. We do not keep a copy.
Advertising ID, device and usage data held by ad and analytics providers (such as Unity, Google AdMob)We ask the provider to delete it on request. Providers may keep limited data for as long as their own policies or the law require.
