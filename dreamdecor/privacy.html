This Privacy Policy explains how Studio 010 collects, uses, and shares information when you play Dream Decor (the "Game"). By downloading or playing the Game, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Game.

1. Information We Collect

a) Information collected automatically

  • Device information: device model, operating system and version, language, screen size, network type, and similar technical details.
  • Identifiers: advertising ID (such as the Google Advertising ID) and an anonymous app or installation ID.
  • Gameplay and usage data: rooms decorated, mini-games played, missions and events completed, in-game currency earned and spent, session length, features used, and crash or performance logs.
  • Approximate location: derived from your IP address (country or region level). We do not collect precise GPS location.
  • Ad interaction data: whether an ad was shown, watched or closed, and basic information about the ad network that served it.

b) Information stored on your device

  • Your game progress, character outfit, settings, and purchase records are saved locally on your device (for example in save files or Unity PlayerPrefs).

c) Purchase information

  • When you buy in-game items or subscribe to Dream VIP, the transaction is processed by Google Play (or the Apple App Store). We receive confirmation of the purchase (such as the product ID, order or transaction ID, and subscription status), but we never receive or store your payment card details.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel them in Google Play (or the App Store) at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your store account settings.

d) Information you send us

  • If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not knowingly collect your name, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Game.
  • To save progress, restore purchases, and manage subscriptions.
  • To show advertisements (including rewarded ads you choose to watch) and measure their performance.
  • To analyze usage, balance gameplay, and fix bugs and crashes.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse and to keep the Game secure.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Advertising Choices and Consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region that requires it, the Game will ask for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice later in the Game's settings where available. You can also limit ad personalization at any time on your device:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Apple Advertising settings.

Opting out does not remove ads; it makes them less personalized. Rewarded ads are always optional: you choose whether to watch them. If you purchase the "No Ads" item or are an active Dream VIP member, forced (interstitial) ads are switched off.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information only:

  • with service providers that help us run the Game (advertising, payments, analytics), under their own terms;
  • when required by law or to protect our rights, users, or safety;
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case you will be informed of any change in ownership or use of your data.

Sharing identifiers with advertising partners so they can show and measure ads may count as "sharing" for advertising under some laws; see "Your Rights" below for how to opt out.

6. Data Retention

Gameplay data stored on your device remains until you delete the Game or clear its data. Data held by us or our providers (such as analytics or ad networks) is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above, then deleted or anonymized.

7. Children's Privacy

Dream Decor is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at raovikas603@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

Depending on where you live (including under GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and similar laws), you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct or delete it;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • request a portable copy of your data;
  • opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for advertising (we do not sell personal information for money).

To exercise these rights, email raovikas603@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

You can also delete locally stored data at any time by uninstalling the Game or clearing its app data. Because the Game has no accounts, most data is tied to your device and advertising ID; resetting your advertising ID separates earlier ad data from you.
