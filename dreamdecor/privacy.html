This Privacy Policy explains how Studio 010 collects, uses, and shares information when you play Dream Decor (the "Game"). By downloading or playing the Game, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Game.

1. Information We Collect

a) Information collected automatically

  • Device information: device model, operating system and version, language, screen size, and similar technical details.
  • Identifiers: advertising ID (such as Google Advertising ID), and an anonymous app or installation ID.
  • Gameplay and usage data: levels played, rooms decorated, missions and events completed, in-game currency earned and spent, session length, features used, and crash or performance logs.
  • Approximate location: derived from your IP address (country or region level). We do not collect precise GPS location.

b) Information stored on your device

  • Your game progress, settings, and purchases are saved locally on your device (for example via Unity PlayerPrefs or save files).

c) Purchase information

  • When you buy in-game items or a subscription, the transaction is processed by Google Play (or the Apple App Store). We receive confirmation of the purchase (such as product ID and receipt), but we never receive or store your payment card details.

d) Information you send us

  • If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not knowingly collect your name, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Game.
  • To save progress, restore purchases, and manage subscriptions.
  • To show advertisements and measure their performance.
  • To analyze usage, balance gameplay, and fix bugs and crashes.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse and to keep the Game secure.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Advertising Choices

You can limit ad personalization at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Apple Advertising settings.

Opting out does not remove ads; it makes them less personalized.
