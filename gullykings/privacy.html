Gully Kings – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026 · Includes online play, friends, rewards and optional Google sign-in

Gully Kings (“the game”, “we”, “us”) is a casual board game for Android published by Studio010. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it short and plain.

1. Who we are and how to contact us

Studio010
Email: raovikas603@gmail.com

2. The short version

  • You can play offline (against bots or friends on one device) without any account. Nothing about those matches leaves your device.
  • If you choose Online, the game signs you in automatically as an anonymous guest and stores a few things on our servers (section 4): a player ID, a display name, your friends list and online match records.
  • Signing in with Google (optional) lets you keep your progress on a new phone. We do not receive your Google name or email address.
  • There are no in-app purchases in this version. The stars you earn in the game are free and have no money value.
  • Your settings and unfinished offline matches stay on your device.
  • The game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service, and its advertising partners (such as Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, AppLovin and Unity Ads). These partners may collect certain device information to show and measure ads (see section 5).
  • We do not sell your personal information.

3. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following on your device, to make the game work:

  • Your settings (for example sound, music, vibration, reduced motion, board theme).
  • An unfinished match, so you can resume it.
  • Simple play statistics (for example how many matches you have played or won), your stars, daily streak and unlocked themes.

This data is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its storage in your Android settings. If you linked Google, a copy of your progress is also kept online (section 4) so you can restore it.

Reminders

The game can show reminders (for example when your daily reward is ready). They are scheduled on your device from your own reward progress; no information is sent to us or to anyone else to do this. On Android 13 and newer the game asks for permission to show notifications after you claim your first daily reward. You can switch reminders off in Settings → Daily reminders or in your Android notification settings.

4. Online play, friends and rewards (Unity Gaming Services)

These features run only if you open Online, Friends or Account. They use Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies) to sign you in and to store data on servers. When you use them, the following is stored:

  • Anonymous player ID. Created automatically the first time you go online. It identifies you in matches and is not linked to your real identity.
  • Display name. A random guest name at first; you can change it. Other players in your matches and your friends can see it. Please do not use your real name or anything that identifies you.
  • Friend code, friends list and invites. A short code that lets other players add you as a friend, the list of friends (their player ID and display name) and room invitations, which expire after 10 minutes.
  • Online match records. Room code, the players’ display names and player IDs, the moves made and when, and the result.
  • Progress backup (only if you link Google). Your match counts, stars, daily streak, unlocked board themes and claimed achievements.

Unity Gaming Services also receives technical information needed to run the service, such as your IP address and device information, and handles it under its own policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Sign in with Google (optional)

If you tap Save with Google, the game asks Google Play Games for a one-time code and gives it to Unity Authentication, which links your anonymous player ID to your Google Play Games account. We do not receive your name, email address or profile picture from Google; the link only lets you sign in to the same player on another phone. You can still play without doing this. Google’s privacy policy applies to your Google account: policies.google.com/privacy.

What we use it for

To run matches and keep them fair, to let you play with friends, to remember your progress if you link Google, and to keep the service secure. We do not use this data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not combine it with advertising data.

5. Advertising (Unity LevelPlay and ad partners)

The game displays banner, interstitial (full-screen) and rewarded (optional, watch-to-earn) ads. To do this it uses Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service, which requests ads from advertising networks. Depending on the version of the game and what is available in your region, these networks may include:

  • Google AdMob (Google);
  • Meta Audience Network (Meta Platforms);
  • AppLovin;
  • Unity Ads (Unity Technologies).

To serve and measure ads, LevelPlay and these partners may collect and use:

  • Your device’s advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • Device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, language, app version, network type);
  • Ad interaction and diagnostic information (such as ads shown, clicked, completed, and crash or performance data).

This information is collected and processed by each company under its own privacy policy:

  • Unity LevelPlay / Unity: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Meta Audience Network: facebook.com/privacy/policy
  • AppLovin: applovin.com/privacy

Ads are shown only on the main menu (banner), after some finished matches (full-screen), and when you choose to watch a rewarded ad. We never show ads in the middle of your turn.

Your ad choices

  • In the game: the first time you open the game you are asked to choose between personalised ads and ads that are not personalised. You can change this at any time in Settings → Personalised ads. Your choice is passed to LevelPlay and its partners. Where required, it also tells them not to sell or share your personal information.
  • Android settings: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by device).

6. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your real name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data. We do not run our own analytics or tracking. There is no chat in the game; other players only see your display name.

7. Children

Gully Kings is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us at the email above and we will help.

8. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the services that run the game: Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services, which stores the online data in section 4), Google (only if you choose Sign in with Google), and Unity LevelPlay and its advertising partners (such as Google, Meta and AppLovin), as described in section 5, for the purpose of showing and measuring ads. Other players see your display name in shared matches and friends lists.

9. Data retention and deletion

Data kept on your device stays until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Online data (section 4) is kept while the game is in use. To have your online data deleted (player ID, display name, friends, match records and progress backup), email us at the address below from the account you use and tell us your display name and friend code, and we will delete it within 30 days. Match records may remain in other players’ match histories in anonymised form. For data held by advertising companies, please use the privacy tools linked in section 5 (for example Google’s tools at myaccount.google.com).

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA/UK, or privacy laws in some US states), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. For the online data in section 4 you can ask us directly. For the advertising data described in section 5 these rights are exercised with the advertising companies listed there. You can contact us at raovikas603@gmail.com with any request or question.

11. Security

Information saved on your device is protected by Android’s app sandboxing. Online data is stored by Unity Gaming Services in private storage that only our server code can read, and it is sent over encrypted connections. No method of electronic storage is perfectly secure, but we only keep what the game needs to run.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game grows (for example if we add new online features or purchases in a future version). We will post the updated policy at this page and change the effective date above. Where the law requires, we will ask for your consent again.
