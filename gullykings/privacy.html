Gully Kings – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026 · Version 0.1.0

Gully Kings (“the game”, “we”, “us”) is a casual board game for Android published by Studio010. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it short and plain.

1. Who we are and how to contact us

Studio010
Email: raovikas603@gmail.com

2. The short version

  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no online multiplayer in this version.
  • There are no in-app purchases in this version.
  • Your game progress and settings stay on your device.
  • The game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service, and its advertising partners (such as Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, AppLovin and Unity Ads). These partners may collect certain device information to show and measure ads (see section 4).
  • We do not sell your personal information.

3. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following only on your device, to make the game work. We do not receive or see it:

  • Your settings (for example sound, music, vibration, reduced motion, board theme).
  • An unfinished match, so you can resume it.
  • Simple play statistics (for example how many matches you have played or won).

This data is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its storage in your Android settings.

4. Advertising (Unity LevelPlay and ad partners)

The game displays banner, interstitial (full-screen) and rewarded (optional, watch-to-earn) ads. To do this it uses Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service, which requests ads from advertising networks. Depending on the version of the game and what is available in your region, these networks may include:

  • Google AdMob (Google);
  • Meta Audience Network (Meta Platforms);
  • AppLovin;
  • Unity Ads (Unity Technologies).

To serve and measure ads, LevelPlay and these partners may collect and use:

  • Your device’s advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • Device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, language, app version, network type);
  • Ad interaction and diagnostic information (such as ads shown, clicked, completed, and crash or performance data).

This information is collected and processed by each company under its own privacy policy:

  • Unity LevelPlay / Unity: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Meta Audience Network: facebook.com/privacy/policy
  • AppLovin: applovin.com/privacy

Ads are shown only on the main menu (banner), after some finished matches (full-screen), and when you choose to watch a rewarded ad. We never show ads in the middle of your turn.

Your ad choices

  • In the game: the first time you open the game you are asked to choose between personalised ads and ads that are not personalised. You can change this at any time in Settings → Personalised ads. Your choice is passed to LevelPlay and its partners. Where required, it also tells them not to sell or share your personal information.
  • Android settings: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by device).

5. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data. We do not run our own analytics or tracking in this version, and the game does not use social login.

6. Children

Gully Kings is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us at the email above and we will help.

7. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it ourselves. The only third parties that receive device information through the game are Unity LevelPlay and its advertising partners (such as Google, Meta and AppLovin), as described in section 4, for the purpose of showing and measuring ads.

8. Data retention and deletion

Data kept on your device stays until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Because we do not hold your personal data on our own servers, we have nothing to delete on our side. For data held by advertising companies, please use the privacy tools linked in section 4 (for example Google’s tools at myaccount.google.com).

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA/UK, or privacy laws in some US states), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Since we do not keep personal data about you, most of these rights are exercised with the advertising companies listed in section 4 for the advertising data described above. You can also contact us at raovikas603@gmail.com with any request or question.

10. Security

Information saved by the game stays on your device and is protected by Android’s app sandboxing. No method of electronic storage is perfectly secure, but we only keep what the game needs to run.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game grows (for example if we add accounts, online play or purchases in a future version). We will post the updated policy at this page and change the effective date above. Where the law requires, we will ask for your consent again.
