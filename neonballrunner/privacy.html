Privacy Policy

Neon Ball Runner · Last updated: 19 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how studio010 ("we", "us", "our") handles information in the mobile game Neon Ball Runner (the "Game"). By playing the Game you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

The Game has no accounts, sign-in, chat or social features. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location or contacts, and we do not collect them ourselves.

Data stored on your device. The Game saves your best score and your settings (such as steering sensitivity, music on/off and vibration on/off) locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

Data collected by advertising partners. The Game shows ads. To serve, personalise, measure and secure those ads, the advertising services described in section 3 may automatically collect and use information such as:

  • Advertising ID (e.g. Android Advertising ID) and other device identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • Device and app information (device model, operating system version, language, screen size, network type, app version);
  • Ad interaction and diagnostic data (ads requested, viewed or clicked, ad performance, crashes, and ad fraud signals).

We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you, and we do not sell it.

2. How the information is used

  • Local data is used only to save your progress and settings.
  • Data collected by advertising partners is used to deliver, personalise and measure ads, to prevent fraud and abuse, and to maintain and improve ad services. Each advertising partner acts as an independent data controller for its own use of this data.

3. Advertising and ad mediation

The Game contains three kinds of ads: banner ads, interstitial (full-screen) ads shown between runs, and rewarded video ads that you can choose to watch to continue a run.

Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service operated by Unity Technologies. LevelPlay requests ads from several advertising networks at the same time and shows the best available ad. The networks we use, or may enable, include:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC);
  • Unity Ads (Unity Technologies);
  • ironSource Ads (Unity Technologies);
  • AppLovin (AppLovin Corporation);
  • Meta Audience Network (Meta Platforms, Inc.).

Which of these networks serve an ad in the Game can change over time. The information listed in section 1 may be shared with Unity LevelPlay and with these networks in order to serve and measure ads. We do not control how these companies use that information. Please read their privacy policies:

  • Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads and ironSource: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • AppLovin: https://www.applovin.com/privacy/
  • Meta Audience Network: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/

Personalised ads and your choices. Ads may be personalised based on your interests. You can control this at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization".
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Apple Advertising settings.

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland). If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent when you first open it, using Google's User Messaging Platform, before any ads are requested. You can choose to consent, manage your options, or decline. Your choice is passed to Unity LevelPlay and the advertising networks. You can change your choice at any time with the privacy button (a shield icon) on the start screen and the pause screen. If you do not consent, ads may still be shown but will not be personalised, or may not be shown, depending on your choice and the law that applies to you.

US state privacy rights. If you live in a US state with a privacy law (for example California), you may have the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. You can limit ad personalisation with your device's advertising ID settings above, or contact us (section 11).

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with Unity LevelPlay and the advertising networks described in section 3, and with authorities if required by law.

5. Third-party services

The Game uses the following third-party software development kits (SDKs), each governed by its own privacy policy listed in section 3: Unity LevelPlay (with its ironSource and Unity Ads networks), Google Mobile Ads and the Google User Messaging Platform. If we enable AppLovin or Meta Audience Network, their SDKs will also apply.

6. Children's privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we do not knowingly show personalised ads to children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will help resolve it.

7. Data retention and deletion

We do not hold personal data on our servers. Locally stored scores and settings can be deleted by clearing the Game's data or uninstalling it. Data held by advertising partners is governed by their policies and the controls described in section 3.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the limited information the Game handles. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to opt out of personalised advertising. Because we do not hold identifiable data, most of these choices are managed through your device settings, the consent privacy button in the Game, and the advertising partners' own privacy controls. You can also contact us with any request.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add or remove an advertising network. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of the Game after a change means you accept the updated policy.
