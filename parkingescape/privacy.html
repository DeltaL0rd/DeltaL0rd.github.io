This Privacy Policy explains how Studio010 ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you play Parking Escape (the "Game") on your Android device. By playing the Game you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the Game.

1. Who we are

The Game is published by Studio010.
Contact: raovikas603@gmail.com

2. Short summary

  • You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device.
  • The Game shows ads provided by third-party advertising partners. These partners may collect certain device information, such as your advertising ID, to deliver and measure ads.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland we ask for your choice about personalised ads before any ad is requested.
  • The Game can send you local reminder notifications. You can turn them off at any time.

3. Information we handle

3.1 Information stored on your device

The Game saves the following on your device only: your level and star progress, coins and hints, purchased worlds and car paints, daily reward status, sound, music and vibration settings, whether you finished the tutorial, your ad privacy choice and your reminder setting. We do not receive this data on our servers. If you uninstall the Game or clear its data, it is deleted.

3.2 Information collected by advertising partners

The Game uses Unity LevelPlay to mediate ads, together with the ad networks connected to it, which currently include Google AdMob and Unity Ads. When an ad is requested or shown, these partners may automatically collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (Google Advertising ID);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and software information (model, operating system version, language, screen size, battery and network status);
  • information about ad interactions, such as ads shown, clicked and completed, and app version and crash or diagnostic information related to ads.

We do not control these partners' data practices. They act under their own privacy policies (see section 8).

3.3 Notifications

The Game schedules local reminder notifications on your device (twice a day). They are created by the Game on your device and do not require a server. On Android 13 and later, the Game asks for permission to show notifications. You can turn reminders off in the Game's Settings or in your device settings.

3.4 Information we do not collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, or payment information. The Game currently has no real-money purchases.

5. Your choices and consent

  • Ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): the first time you open the Game in these regions, a privacy prompt asks whether you agree to personalised ads. If you decline, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad Privacy Choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Notifications: turn them off in the Game's Settings (Reminders) or in your device's app notification settings.
  • Delete your data: uninstall the Game or clear its storage in your device settings. This removes the data stored on your device.

6. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where data protection law such as the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we and our advertising partners process information about ads on the basis of your consent (for personalised ads and the use of your advertising ID), and of our legitimate interests in providing a free game and keeping it secure (for non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and basic measurement). You may withdraw your consent at any time as described in section 5. Withdrawing does not affect processing done before.

Because we do not hold your personal data on our own servers, most requests relate to our advertising partners. You can contact them directly or write to us at raovikas603@gmail.com and we will help as far as we can.

California and other US states: we do not sell personal information for money. Ad partners may use advertising identifiers in ways that some laws call "sharing" or "targeted advertising". You can opt out by turning off ads personalisation in your device settings.
