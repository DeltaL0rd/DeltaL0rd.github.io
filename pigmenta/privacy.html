Privacy Policy

PIGMENTA: Color Alchemy · Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you play PIGMENTA: Color Alchemy (the “game”), a colour-mixing puzzle for Android, published by studio010 (“we”, “us”). We keep things simple: the game needs no account, and we do not run our own servers that collect your data.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or microphone. There is no sign-in, no chat and no user-generated content.

2. Information stored on your device

Your progress (completed levels, stars, coins, unlocked items, daily challenge status) and your settings (sound, music, vibration, reminders, privacy choice) are saved only on your device. We never receive this data. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it.

3. Advertising

The game is free and shows ads: rewarded videos you can choose to watch (for example, to get a hint), interstitial ads between levels, and a banner on menu screens. Ads are never shown in the middle of a puzzle.

To show ads we use Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies / ironSource) as an ad mediation service, together with ad networks such as Unity Ads and Google AdMob. These companies act as independent providers and may collect and process the following through the ad SDKs:

3a. Reminder notifications

The game can send you up to two reminder notifications a day: a morning message when the daily puzzle and its reward are ready, and an evening nudge. Today’s evening message is skipped once you have finished the daily puzzle. To keep things gentle, reminders are scheduled only a week ahead and are refreshed each time you play, so if you stop playing the reminders stop too.

These are local notifications: they are created and scheduled by the game on your own device. They do not use a server, a push-notification token or your account, and no information about you leaves your device to send them. On Android 13 and newer the game asks for your permission before sending any notification. You can turn reminders off at any time with the Reminders switch in the game’s Settings, or in your phone’s Settings > Apps > PIGMENTA > Notifications. Declining does not affect the game in any way.

4. Your ad choices

  • European Economic Area and United Kingdom: when you first open the game, Google’s consent form appears. You can accept, decline or manage your choices. Ads are requested only as your choice allows.
  • Everywhere else: the game asks you to choose between personalized and non-personalized ads. You get the same game either way.
  • Change your mind at any time: open Settings > Privacy choices in the game.
  • Android settings: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in Settings > Privacy > Ads on your phone.

Even with non-personalised ads, providers may still process some data (for example, to limit how often an ad is shown, to measure ads and to prevent fraud).

5. Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help.

6. Legal bases (EEA / UK)

Where the GDPR applies, ad providers rely on your consent for personalised advertising, which you can withdraw at any time as described above. Other ad processing (such as fraud prevention and measurement) is based on the providers’ legitimate interests as described in their policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold your data, requests about advertising data should be sent to the relevant ad provider (see section 3). You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Data sharing and transfers

We do not sell or share your personal data. Ad providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under safeguards they describe in their own policies.

9. Security

Your game data stays on your device and is protected by Android’s app sandbox. Ad data is protected by the providers’ own security measures.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add features. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be announced in the game or on the store page.
