Privacy Policy

Game: POURFECT! – Draw, Pour & Solve (Android package com.studio010.pourfect)
Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains what information the game POURFECT! (the "Game") uses, why, and what choices you have. The Game is published by Studio010 ("we", "us"). If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the Game.

1. The short version

  • You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device.
  • The Game is free and shows ads. The ad companies we work with may collect certain device information to show and measure ads.
  • If you are in the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland, we ask for your choice about personalised ads before any ad is loaded. You can change your choice at any time.
  • The Game has no real-money purchases. Extra hints are earned with in-game coins or by watching an optional rewarded ad.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress locally (for example: current level in each mode, stars, coins, hint count, daily gift status, sound settings, and your ad-privacy choice). This data stays on your device. We do not receive it, and it is removed when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

3. Advertising and the information ad partners collect

The Game displays banner, interstitial (full-screen) and rewarded video ads. To do this we use Unity LevelPlay (a mediation service by Unity / ironSource), which can load ads from partner networks such as Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, these companies may automatically collect:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) and device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, operating system version, language, app version, network type)
  • Ad interaction data (ads shown, clicked, watched) and diagnostic/performance data

This information is used to deliver ads, limit how often you see them, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and (if you agreed) personalise ads. These companies act under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity / LevelPlay: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/privacy

4. Your choices

  • Consent (EU/EEA/UK/Switzerland): on first launch you are asked to accept or decline personalised ads. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised using your data. You can change your choice any time in Settings → ADS PRIVACY.
  • Reset your advertising ID: on your Android device go to Settings → Privacy → Ads to delete or reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation.
  • Delete local data: uninstall the Game or clear its storage in your device settings.

5. Legal bases (EEA / UK)

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on your consent for personalised advertising, and on our legitimate interest in running a free game and showing non-personalised ads, preventing fraud and keeping the Game working.

6. Sharing and international transfers

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the advertising partners described above, which may process it in countries outside your own (including the United States) under safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

7. Data retention

We keep no personal data about you on our own servers. Ad partners keep information for the periods stated in their own policies.

8. Children's privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the Game, please contact us and we will help resolve it.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to processing, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with your data-protection authority. Because we do not hold personal data about you ourselves, most requests are best directed to the ad partners listed above; you may also contact us and we will help where we can.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the Game and the data it handles. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.
