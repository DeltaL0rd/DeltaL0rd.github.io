Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

1. Who we are

WaterColors (the "Game") is a colour-mixing puzzle game for Android published by studio010 ("we", "us", "our"). If you have any question about this policy, you can contact us at raovikas603@gmail.com.

2. The short version

  • You do not need an account to play. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or contact list.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device. We do not receive it.
  • The Game is free and shows ads. Our advertising partners may collect certain device information (such as your advertising ID) to show and measure ads.
  • You choose between personalized and non-personalized ads the first time you open the Game, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • The Game is not directed to children under 13.

3. Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the Game saves the following data locally on your phone: your level progress and star ratings, your Golden Keys balance, daily reward and daily goal progress, achievements and statistics, timed challenge best scores, your settings (sound, music, vibration, tips, screen shake), the items you have purchased, and your privacy choice. This information stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the Game or clear its storage.

5. Advertising and your choices

  • In the Game: when you first open the Game we ask whether you prefer personalized or non-personalized ads. You can review or change this at any time under Settings → Privacy choices. If you choose non-personalized ads, you will still see ads, but they will not be tailored using your advertising ID or interests.
  • On your device: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your Android settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Remove ads: you can buy "Remove Ads" or the "Studio Pass" in the Shop. Once purchased, the Game no longer shows banner or interstitial ads. Optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a bonus are not shown unless you tap them.

6. In-app purchases

Purchases of Golden Key packs, Remove Ads and the Studio Pass are processed by Google Play. We receive confirmation that a purchase was completed so we can unlock the item. We do not receive or store your card or other payment details.

7. How we use information

The information handled by the Game is used only to: run the Game and save your progress on your device; show ads and let you earn rewards for watching them; process and restore your purchases; keep the Game secure and prevent fraud or abuse; and comply with the law. We do not sell your personal information.

8. Sharing of information

We do not share personal information with anyone ourselves, because the Game does not send your information to our servers. Information collected by the advertising and purchase services described above is shared with those service providers as described in their policies. We may also disclose information if required by law.

9. Data retention

Game data stays on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its storage. Data held by third-party services is kept according to their own retention policies.

10. Children's privacy

WaterColors is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the Game, please contact us at raovikas603@gmail.com and we will help resolve it.
